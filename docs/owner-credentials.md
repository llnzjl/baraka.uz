# Owner account settings

The owner account has no email sign-in, email verification, or email password-reset flow. Sign in with the owner username and password, then use **My Page** to update the username, display name, and phone number. To change the password, enter the current password and the new password there. The current owner session remains active, and other owner sessions are invalidated after changes.

If the owner no longer knows the current password, recover access through the trusted database operator who manages the restaurant deployment. Owner account recovery does not send email.

Optional reservation/customer email notifications are separate from owner account access and continue to use the Resend settings when configured.
